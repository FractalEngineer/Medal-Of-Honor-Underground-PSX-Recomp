# v0.1.6 - Full 6dof VR + Mouse/Keyboard Alpha

This patch centres aiming and removes the weapon-swap jerk:

- The aiming reticle, the shot target and the camera photograph target now sit
  at the true centre of the screen for every weapon and item, including the
  mounted machine gun.
- The reticle follows the live display resolution, so higher internal
  resolutions and widescreen sizes keep the same screen-centre aim point.
- Cycling weapons while the sniper scope or camera viewfinder is aimed no
  longer jerks or teleports the player. The aimed zoom carries into the new
  weapon instead of easing back out.

The release retains the VR mode, configurable mouse and keyboard controls,
aimed zoom for firearms, the crossbow and both throwables, native objective
camera aiming, the frontend menu input fix, mounted-gun aiming and packaged
flat and VR launchers from v0.1.5. See the
[v0.1.5 notes](https://github.com/FractalEngineer/Medal-Of-Honor-Underground-PSX-Recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.5)
for those features.

Download `mohu-0.1.6-windows-x64.zip` and extract the whole archive. Use
`RunFlat.bat` for flat play, then enable **Mouse and keyboard** in **Mods**.
Connect a headset and use `RunVR-VDXR.bat`, `RunVR-SteamVR.bat` or
`RunVR-Oculus.bat` for VR.

Supply your own **SLUS-01270 (NTSC-U)** CUE/BIN image. No game data or retail
BIOS is included. Keep your existing `saves/` folder when updating; memory-card
saves are separate from executable-specific savestates. See
[ALPHA_README.md](../../ALPHA_README.md) for setup and current limitations.

This remains a Windows alpha. Broader campaign, weapon and headset coverage
still needs playtesting.
