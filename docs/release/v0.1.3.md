# v0.1.3 - Full 6dof VR + Mouse/Keyboard Alpha

This patch improves mouse and keyboard controls in the single-player campaign:

- Mounted guns follow mouse movement horizontally and vertically. Their firing
  direction aligns with the view, and the left/right movement bindings traverse
  the gun within its native limits.
- Weapon cycling in either direction skips empty weapons the game cannot select,
  while retaining selectable weapons with ammunition or native no-ammo behavior.

The release retains the VR mode, configurable mouse and keyboard controls,
aimed zoom and packaged flat and VR launchers from v0.1.2. See the
[v0.1.2 notes](https://github.com/FractalEngineer/Medal-Of-Honor-Underground-PSX-Recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.2)
for those features.

Download `mohu-0.1.3-windows-x64.zip` and extract the whole archive. Use
`RunFlat.bat` for flat play, then enable **Mouse and keyboard** in **Mods**.
Connect a headset and use `RunVR-VDXR.bat`, `RunVR-SteamVR.bat` or
`RunVR-Oculus.bat` for VR.

Supply your own **SLUS-01270 (NTSC-U)** CUE/BIN image. No game data or retail
BIOS is included. Keep your existing `saves/` folder when updating; memory-card
saves are separate from executable-specific savestates. See
[ALPHA_README.md](../../ALPHA_README.md) for setup and current limitations.

This remains a Windows alpha. Broader campaign, weapon and headset coverage
still needs playtesting.
